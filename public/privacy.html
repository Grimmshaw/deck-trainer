Privacy policy

Lanterna · Last updated: 14 October 2026

Lanterna (“the app”) is published by Lanterna Konsult AB (reg. no. 559568-9893), Nunnebanan 30, 618 95 Stavsjö, Sweden (“we”). This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: we do not collect any personal data, unless you choose to send us a problem report. The app sends anonymous usage statistics that cannot be linked to you.

No account, no tracking

The app works without an account or login. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools, and it does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Anonymous usage statistics

To see whether the app is used and which parts are useful, the app tells our own server (hosted by Netlify) when it is opened (at most once a day), when an exercise is started and when it is finished. Each message contains only: what happened, the study category and type of exercise, the result in tens of percent, the app version and the kind of device (for example “iPhone” or “Google Play app”). The server adds the country, worked out from the connection. We do not store your IP address, and the app sends no name, account, device identifier or cookie, so the statistics cannot be linked to you or to your device. They are only used to improve the app and are only seen by us.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, your chosen region and your study progress (which questions you have answered right or wrong) are saved only in the app’s local storage on your own device. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Problem reports

If you choose to send a problem report from the app, we receive what you write, the reason you pick, the question it is about (with the right answer and the answer you chose) and the app version. The app adds nothing else – no name, e-mail address or device identifier – but our hosting provider (Netlify Forms) also records the IP address and browser type with each report, as web servers do. Reports are only used to fix the app and are deleted when they have been dealt with. If you send the report by e-mail instead, we also receive your e-mail address, and use it only to reply to you.

Deleting your data

You do not need an account to use Lanterna, so there is no account to delete. What you can do:

  • Data on your device (settings, progress, purchase status): clear the app’s data or uninstall the app. A purchase on Google Play is kept by Google and comes back when you reinstall.
  • Problem reports: e-mail robin_rantala@lanternakonsult.se with the subject “Lanterna data deletion” and roughly when you sent the report and what it was about. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.
  • Anonymous usage statistics contain nothing that can be linked to you or your device, so we cannot find or delete statistics from a particular person. They are kept only as long as they are useful for improving the app.

Hosting

The web version of the app is delivered by a hosting provider (Netlify). Like any web server, the provider may briefly record technical information such as IP address and browser type in server logs, to deliver the app and keep the service secure. We do not use this information to identify you.

Purchases

If you buy access to extra content in the version on Google Play, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google’s own privacy policy. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, but never your payment details.

Children

The app is made for maritime students and is not directed at children under 13. Since we collect no personal data, no data about children is collected either.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy – for example if we add a feature that sends data – we will update this page before the change takes effect and show the new date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: robin_rantala@lanternakonsult.se
